**Selected Facts:**

ru Facts:
1. Duck meat and skin are served with young onions, cut into tassel shapes.
2. The process of preparation and frying takes about two days.
3. The duck is ordered whole at restaurants.
4. The soup of Chinese cabbage is served after the meat.
5. When serving the duck, the carcass is cut into 80-120 thin slices.

fr Facts:
1. The preparation of lacquered Peking duck begins at the birth of the animal.
2. The duck is then inflated with air under the skin.
3. The serving of this dish follows the ceremonial culinary art inherited from the Chinese gastronomic tradition.
4. The skin of the lacquered duck is presented on a plate, in thin slices made up of the lacquered and crispy skin, along with a bit of fat and meat.

zh Facts:
1. The duck is whole when roasted.
2. After the duck is placed in the oven, it is necessary to use a skewer to regularly change the position of the duck.
3. One type is hanging oven roasted duck.
4. All the roast duck shops have switched to using lean meat ducks.

**Quiz Questions:**

**Question 1:** How is Peking duck typically served to diners in restaurants?
Options:
A. As a whole duck
B. In large pieces
C. As duck soup
D. With duck's feet only

Answer: A. As a whole duck

**Question 2:** What unique preparation step is done to the duck before roasting it for Peking duck?
Options:
A. It is marinated in soy sauce
B. It is inflated with air under the skin
C. It is stuffed with rice
D. It is boiled in water

Answer: B. It is inflated with air under the skin

**Question 3:** How long does the preparation and frying process of Peking duck take?
Options:
A. One day
B. Two days
C. Three hours
D. Five hours

Answer: B. Two days

**Question 4:** What is served after the meat in a traditional Peking duck meal?
Options:
A. Rice
B. Noodles
C. Chinese cabbage soup
D. Dessert

Answer: C. Chinese cabbage soup

**Question 5:** What type of oven is one method of roasting Peking duck?
Options:
A. Brick oven
B. Stone oven
C. Hanging oven
D. Microwave oven

Answer: C. Hanging oven

**Question 6:** What is a notable characteristic of the duck meat used by modern roast duck shops?
