**Question:** In which city did the first restaurant specializing in pho open during the 1920s?
Options:
A. Ho Chi Minh City
B. Hanoi
C. Da Nang
D. Hue

**Answer:** B. Hanoi

**Question:** Which region of Vietnam is known for preparing a sweeter version of pho?
Options:
A. Northern Vietnam
B. Central Vietnam
C. Southern Vietnam
D. Western Vietnam

**Answer:** C. Southern Vietnam

**Question:** According to some researchers, pho may have originated from which Cantonese dish?
Options:
A. Wonton noodle soup
B. Shahefen
C. Congee
D. Chow mein

**Answer:** B. Shahefen

**Question:** Which ingredient can be used to garnish pho in the southern part of Vietnam?
Options:
A. Fish sauce
B. Tuong (Vietnamese soy paste)
C. Coriander
D. Basil

**Answer:** B. Tuong (Vietnamese soy paste)

**Question:** What is the alternative name for Saigon-style pho?

**Question:** When raw beef is served in pho, how is it usually cooked?
